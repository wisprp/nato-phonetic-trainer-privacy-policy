Privacy Policy

NATO Phonetic Trainer — Android app (me.scheria.nato_phonetic_trainer)
Effective date: 28 October 2026 (app version 1.1.0 and later)

Summary

This app does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. There is nothing to delete, export or opt out of, because nothing is gathered in the first place.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in or profile — the app opens straight to the alphabet.
  • No personal data is collected: no name, e-mail, phone number, contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.
  • No analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking libraries are compiled into the app.
  • No network access from this app: the reference content is bundled inside it, and the app holds no internet permission, so it works in airplane mode. It requests no user-facing Android permissions.
  • No data is sent to me or to anyone else — there is no server behind this app.

Spoken pronunciation

Tapping a letter hands that single word to the text-to-speech engine already installed on your device (Android's system speech service). This app does not record audio, does not store anything about what you tapped, and cannot send anything anywhere — it has no internet permission. The speech engine is a separate system component with its own privacy policy: some engines can optionally use cloud voices for languages they have not downloaded on-device. That behaviour belongs to the engine, is not controlled by this app, and sending the word “Alpha” to it involves no personal data.

Information on your device

The app has nothing to remember yet. If a later version stores anything locally (for example a practice score), it stays on your device, is never uploaded, and can be removed by clearing the app storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. Any such change is described here before that version is published.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and up. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling data, this page will be updated and the effective date above changed before the new version reaches Google Play. History: 19 October 2026 — first version; 28 October 2026 — spoken pronunciation added (app 1.1.0), no change to what is collected, which is nothing.
