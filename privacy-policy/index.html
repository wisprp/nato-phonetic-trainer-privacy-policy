Privacy Policy

NATO Phonetic Trainer — Android app (me.scheria.nato_phonetic_trainer)
Effective date: 12 November 2026 (app versions after 1.5.0)

Summary

This app does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. There is nothing to delete, export or opt out of, because nothing is gathered in the first place.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in or profile — the app opens straight to its start screen.
  • No personal data is collected: no name, e-mail, phone number, contacts, location, camera, voice recordings or files.
  • No analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking libraries are compiled into the app.
  • No network access from this app: the reference content is bundled inside it, and the app holds no internet permission, so it works in airplane mode. The only Android permission it asks for is the microphone, and only when you start speaking practice (see below).
  • No data is sent to me or to anyone else — there is no server behind this app.

Spoken pronunciation

Tapping a letter hands that single word to the text-to-speech engine already installed on your device (Android's system speech service). This app does not record audio, does not store anything about what you tapped, and cannot send anything anywhere — it has no internet permission. The speech engine is a separate system component with its own privacy policy: some engines can optionally use cloud voices for languages they have not downloaded on-device. That behaviour belongs to the engine, is not controlled by this app, and sending the word “Alpha” to it involves no personal data.

Speaking practice (microphone)

In Practice speaking the app shows a letter and you say its code word. When you tap the microphone button the app asks for the microphone permission; you can refuse it, and the rest of the app works without it. After the first tap, each next round listens on its own until the practice ends, you leave the screen, or nothing is heard.

Your speech is turned into text by your phone's on-device speech recognizer (a system component of Android, e.g. Android System Intelligence on Pixel phones). This app only uses the on-device recognizer, never the one that may send audio to a server, so the audio is processed on your phone. The app receives the recognised text only (for example “Bravo”), compares it with the expected code word, shows it in the round's result and forgets it when you leave the practice. No audio or text is stored or sent anywhere by this app. If the English speech model is missing, the app may ask Android to download it; that download is done by the system, not by this app, which still has no internet permission.

Information on your device

The app has nothing to remember yet. If a later version stores anything locally (for example a practice score), it stays on your device, is never uploaded, and can be removed by clearing the app storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. Any such change is described here before that version is published.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and up. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling data, this page will be updated and the effective date above changed before the new version reaches Google Play.
